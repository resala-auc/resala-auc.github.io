To the Hearts Behind Resala

Dear Resala Family,

From being a member to becoming Resala’s President, this organization has been one of the biggest journeys of my life. I have sacrificed a lot, but gained even more.

With every role came greater responsibility, not only towards our beneficiaries but also towards every person behind the scenes. I carried your responsibilities with me, and one of my biggest fears was letting any of you down or allowing your efforts to go to waste. There were moments when I struggled to keep going, but whenever my energy ran low, I reminded myself of the people who were counting on us, and of YOU, the team I trusted to stand behind the hope we gave them and the responsibilities we chose to carry.

One of the greatest lessons I learned is that leadership was never about being the best; it was about growing together. What is the value of being the best if we lose the spirit of volunteerism that brought us here?

I genuinely want to thank each of you for every hour, every sacrifice, and every effort you gave Resala. Even the contributions that went unnoticed mattered, and this organization would not be what it is today without you.

I know I haven't always gotten things right. I may have disappointed some of you or left you with experiences you wish had been different. I cannot change those moments, but I can learn from them and make sure I do better. After all, we may forget what people did for us, but we don't always forget how they made us feel. I sincerely hope that, despite my shortcomings, I've left you with more good memories than bad ones.

As you reflect on this journey, be honest about what you gave, what you learned, and whether your heart is still in it.

Resala was never about titles or positions. It was always about people, the spirit we shared, and the good we left behind. Whatever comes next, leave people better than you found them.

With love and gratitude,

Youssef Ibrahim

Resala President

Before You Begin

As we prepare for the upcoming semester, it is important to acknowledge that Resala may undergo changes to its organizational structure and hierarchy. Some positions may be modified, merged, or removed based on the organization's evolving needs.

Continuity in a Board position is not automatic.

Decisions regarding the next semester's structure and team composition will be made by the Presidential Team, taking into consideration semester reports, individual progress, organizational needs, and four main factors:

  • 1

    Ownership

    Taking responsibility, following through on commitments, and treating the role as something you genuinely own.

  • 2

    Leadership

    Taking initiative, supporting others, making decisions, and contributing to the team's overall growth.

  • 3

    Self-Awareness & Commitment

    Recognizing your strengths and shortcomings, being open to feedback, and demonstrating genuine commitment to Resala.

  • 4

    Skills

    Having or developing the skills and capabilities required to fulfill the responsibilities of the role.

This form is an opportunity to reflect honestly on your experience, recognize what went well, acknowledge what could have been better, and share your intentions for the future.

This form is only here to help us understand where you are right now. It will not affect any decisions; those will be based on the experience we shared with you throughout the last semester.
