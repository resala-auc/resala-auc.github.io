Dear AUC students,

This year, Resala AUC reached [ 0,000 ] families, taught [ 000 ] children every
Saturday, ran [ 00 ] initiatives, and did all of it with [ 000 ] volunteers.

Every one of those numbers is a person. A family that opened their door. A
child who learned something on a Saturday they will still have at thirty.

And every one of them happened because somebody decided to show up. A member is
the person who is there: packing, teaching, filming, planning, carrying. It is
why any of those numbers exist.

You can read that list and feel good about it. Or you can be in it next year.
Next year's numbers have not been written yet. They will be written by whoever
joins now. You do not need experience — you need to turn up.

Eight committees are taking members:
• Tech Team               • PR & Fundraising
• Operations              • Visits
• Branding & Media        • Children's Day
• HR                      • Initiatives

Each committee has sub-committees inside it, so you choose the actual work, not
just the name. Three questions, then you pick your own 15-minute interview slot.
If you cannot commit to a committee, apply as a general volunteer instead — no
interview, and you come to the events you can make.

Next year, someone will read these numbers and be moved by them.
You can be the reason one of them went up.
