Dear AUC students,

This is what Resala AUC did this year:

Every one of those numbers is a person. A family that opened their door. A
child who learned something on a Saturday they will still have at thirty.

And every one of them happened because somebody decided to show up.

You can read that list and feel good about it. That is allowed. Or you can be
in it next year — because next year's numbers have not been written yet, and
they will be written by whoever joins now.

A member is not the person who signs things off. A member is the person who is
there: packing, teaching, filming, planning, carrying. The work is not glamorous
and it is not always convenient. It is why any of the numbers above exist.

You do not need experience. You need to turn up.

Eight committees are taking members:
  - Tech Team               - PR & Fundraising
  - Operations              - Visits
  - Branding & Media        - Children's Day
  - HR                      - Initiatives

Each one has sub-committees inside it, so you choose the actual work, not just
the name. Three questions, then you pick your own 15-minute interview slot.

Cannot commit to a committee?
Apply as a general volunteer. No committee, no weekly commitment, no interview
— you come to the events you can make and help on the day. It is not a lesser
answer. It is an honest one, and it is welcome.

Open to all AUC students
