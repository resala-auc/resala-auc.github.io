Every one of those numbers is a person. A family that opened their door. A child who learned something on a Saturday they will still have at thirty.

And every one of them happened because somebody decided to show up.

You can read that list and feel good about it. Or you can be in it next year.
Next year’s numbers have not been written yet. They will be written by whoever joins now.

A member is not the person who signs things off. A member is the person who is there: packing, teaching, filming, planning, carrying. The work is not glamorous and it is not always convenient. It is why any of the numbers above exist.

You do not need experience. You need to turn up.

Eight committees are taking members:

• Tech Team
• Operations
• Branding & Media
• HR 		• PR & Fundraising
• Visits
• Children’s Day
• Initiatives

Each one has sub-committees inside it, so you choose the actual work, not just the name. Three questions, then you pick your own 15-minute interview slot.

Cannot commit to a committee?
Apply as a general volunteer. No committee, no weekly commitment, no interview — you come to the events you can make and help on the day. It is not a lesser answer. It is an honest one, and it is welcome.
Next year, someone will read these numbers and be moved by them.
You can be the reason one of them went up.
