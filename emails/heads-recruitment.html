Dear AUC students,

“Ana maly.” Not my problem. It is the sentence that keeps a family exactly where they are.

Resala exists because a few people refused to say it. Every step we build is carried by someone who decided it was their problem.

A head is that someone. You take one part of the work. One system, one team, one promise to a family waiting on the other side. Then you make sure it actually happens. Not perfectly. Just truly.

You do not need experience. You need to finish what you start.
If you are reading this and thinking “I am probably not the right person”, that thought is not a disqualifier. It is usually the first sign that you will take it seriously.

Eight teams are hiring heads:

• Tech
• Operations
• Branding & Media
• HR 		• PR & Fundraising
• Visits
• Children’s Day
• Initiatives

The application walks you through the head roles inside each team, so you can pick the one that fits you.

A year from now, someone will have had a better year because of a step you built.
They will never know your name. You will know.
