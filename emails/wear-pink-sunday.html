This week is Breast Cancer Awareness Week, and Resala has always been about standing with our community. One of the simplest ways to do that is to be seen doing it.
Mandatory for the whole club
Sunday, 18 October
Everyone wears pink.

Wearing pink is not about the clothes. When a whole club turns up in the same colour, people notice, they ask why, and that conversation is how awareness spreads. It is a small act, but it tells everyone around us that we care about the people who are fighting this, and the families standing beside them.

So on Sunday, we ask every member, director and head to wear something pink. A shirt, a scarf, a ribbon or a hijab all count. Any shade works.

Supporting our community is what Resala is about. Let us show it together.
